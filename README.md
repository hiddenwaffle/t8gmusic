# That '80s Game: the soundtrack

The eleven tracks of That '80s Game, by hiddenwaffle. Each plays with a live score that follows the music, and each has a full score to print.

Open `index.html` in a browser, from this repo's GitHub Pages site or from a downloaded copy. It needs nothing from the network: the player, the sound and every score are inside the file.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The player |
| `scores/` | The full scores as PDFs, one a track and all of them in `soundtrack_scores.pdf` |
| [OFL.txt](OFL.txt) | The fonts' licence |

## Rights

## Fonts

The fonts are not part of the game. The page embeds Oswald, Old Standard TT, Bangers and UnifrakturMaguntia, and the PDFs embed EB Garamond and LilyPond's Emmentaler. All are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; their copyright notices and the licence are in [OFL.txt](OFL.txt).
